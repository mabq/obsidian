# Omarchy

**The good**
- UX — there are lot of niceties that would take a lot of work to reproduce.
- Exposure — this guys are on top of the game, new stuff comes out every day and Omarchy is making sure it includes the best out-of-the-box.
- Community and sponsors — stuff like Apple hardware support out-of-the-box.

**The bad**
- Imperative approach — I need to find a way to reproduce the changes I make to the system.
