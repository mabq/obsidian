# Filesystem

An on-disk filesystem structure is what dictates how a storage drive organizes, tracks, and retrieves data.

> [!info]
> Creating a filesystem directly on a raw, unpartitioned disk, is possible but non-common in practice. [[partitions]] ls

Without a filesystem, a drive is just a massive, chaotic sea of raw bits (0s and 1s). The filesystem carves that sea into manageable chunks and builds a database to keep track of what lives where.
